Results
-------
The selected lunar-derived UTC, additional correction and formal UTC
uncertainty are shown first. Solution letters identify the same candidates
throughout Results and Calculations. All mathematical alternatives remain
available. Results rounds UTC to 0.1 second, including carries across midnight;
the numerical result and detailed trail retain full precision.

The accuracy check is visible on the same page. Its UTC is the entered time
plus the existing clock correction, without the additional lunar-derived
correction. Its reference coordinates start from saved DR when available and
can be edited. Uncertain DR or UTC contributes to the discrepancy; it cannot
be treated as sextant error alone. The signed error is model-corrected observed
LD minus true LD, in arcminutes. Positive means the observation is too large.

Positions from the measured altitudes are labelled by time basis: entered UTC
and the selected lunar-derived UTC. Changing reference coordinates updates
the independent distance check, without fitting those coordinates or changing
the derived positions. A near-zero fitted residual at the derived solution is
not an independent accuracy check.

Calculations
------------
Overview: original readings and times, existing clock correction, forward
check of all three observations at the selected solution.
Almanac and corrections: provider and exact epoch, Moon/body GHA, declination,
HP and SD, decimal degrees and navigation notation, instrument/atmosphere,
and Hs through Ho for the simultaneous Direct Triangle comparison. Raw,
apparent, spherical-cleared and WGS84 geocentric distances have distinct labels.
Separately timed observations show their individual corrected epochs rather
than an invalid simultaneous reduction.
UTC search: search interval, scan spacing, tolerance, nearby coarse evaluations,
actual refinement brackets and the link from correction to the Results UTC.
Detailed trail: complete existing arithmetic and evaluations, followed by the
independent accuracy check. Copy report and Save lunar solution retain it.

Selecting a completed candidate rebuilds its report from the retained solve
and immutable ephemeris. It does not rerun the UTC search. In particular it no
longer enters an Android worker modal loop while constructing Results.

Android uses direct candidate data and vertical touch cards, rather than
reading values back from hidden desktop list controls. Calculations has its
own section picker, with independently scrolling content pages. The recorded
sight, global clock correction, input uncertainties and solver are unchanged.

Validation results and review captures are kept locally under
output/celnav-2.9.9, outside this source worktree.
