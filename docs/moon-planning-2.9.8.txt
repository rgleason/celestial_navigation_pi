Celestial Navigation 2.9.8 development fixes
==========================================

Moon visibility in planning (#354)
---------------------------------
Bob's report:
https://github.com/rgleason/celestial_navigation_pi/issues/354#issuecomment-6098100618

The shared planner applies a 1% Moon illumination cutoff to sight
recommendations. This is a planning rule; actual visibility also depends on
sky conditions. The comparison uses the unrounded illuminated fraction.

Below 1%, the Moon remains in the body table with grey text and its illumination
percentage and explanation in Why. It is excluded from recommended two- and
three-body fixes. Lunar pairs remain listed in both the Sun, Moon and Sight
Planner and Lunar Distance and Sextant Tools, with grey text and an explicit
low-illumination caution. The caution also prevents unsuitable observing
windows. Normal visibility and eligibility return at or above 1%.

Both planners display Moon illumination to one decimal place, including the
Android summaries and the body-table CSV. The phase calculation is unchanged.

Validation
----------
The regression tests cover the unrounded cutoff, Moon assessment, pair/triad
exclusion, Bob's 2026-10-10 13:05:16 and 13:05:26 UTC timestamps, both sides of
new Moon, retained lunar pairs, observing-window cautions, and recovery on
2026-10-12. The focused planning selection passes 15 tests.

A task-only desktop probe exercises the real controls with Classic and Compact
calculations, checking grey text, recovery, retained rows, matching percentage
precision, and exclusion from fix recommendations. Both runs pass. The probe
and screenshots are in output/celnav-2.9.8-moon in the parent OpenCPN workspace.

The plugin and test executable build with CMake/Ninja. Optional DE440s data and
the existing public validation fixtures are linked locally for the wider tests.
The UTC, Europe/London, America/New_York and Asia/Kolkata numerical checks pass.
The complete CTest run passes all 12 checks, including the main regression
executable, metadata, package integrity, bundled guides and UTF-8 UI literals.
