Enhanced Accuracy Add-ons window combines DE440s installation/verification
status, verified download (31.2 MiB), offline import and explicit re-verification

opening the additional view. Reopening Eclipse refreshes shared data status.
The DUT1 content panel is shared between both windows, with one scrolling
viewport per host. Android preserves source text while wrapping after rotation,
has touch-sized actions, and uses its normal Close/Back surface.

Planner
-------
Best Sights and the body catalogue are visible together in a resizable
desktop split view. Body sorting is independent of recommendation mode.
Practical Fix, Bright Bodies and Lunar Candidates remain available; old
Show All preferences migrate to alphabetical display with Practical Fix.
Active Hc limits and the Polaris preference are reported even while the
optional settings are collapsed. Empty recommendations explain exclusions.

Full catalogue and Custom are separate display choices. The searchable,
grouped checklist offers all 66 Sun, Moon, planet and star catalogue bodies.
Selections apply to both sky plots, the body table and recommendation pool.
Custom overrides the plot magnitude limit; below-horizon switches retain
their individual display scopes. Empty custom selections persist. Cancel
retains the previous selection. Lunar companions can use any selected body;
the Moon remains the calculation reference even when unchecked for display.
Unavailable bodies remain grey, including the thin Moon. Planning order
keeps the daytime Moon near other bright bodies so it remains discoverable.

The Android planner retains touch cards and a section picker. Bodies has a
direct Best Sights / sky action, a full/custom selector, and collapsible body
options. Recommendations appear before optional fix settings. Native page
changes reflow labels after Qt lays out the visible viewport; direct navigation
also updates the section caption. The custom checklist has its own expanding
touch viewport and responsive columns, rather than a nested form scroll.
Search reads the native QLineEdit text because the pinned wxQt getter retained
an empty value during editing on the tablet. Desktop search remains unchanged.
Astronomical calculations and scoring weights are unchanged.

The whole desktop planner page scrolls vertically only, so focus on a wide
toolbar control cannot scroll headings and table edges off the left. Tables
retain their own horizontal scrolling. The Moon-path checkbox explicitly says
SHA/Dec: it shows orbital motion relative to stars in that view. Local sky uses
the -1 h / +1 h context controls to advance the entire horizon-relative sky.
