We will use the following example to demonstrate How to use the plugin.

Use Case 1 SW of Hawaii UTC 16 Jul 2025

A Cruise SW of Hawaii. •Course 255°T, Speed 6 knots. No set or drift. •Height of Eye (HOE) is 3.0 meters. •UTC July 16, 2025 at ~06:00:00 the ETA for the evening sight. •Estimated DR is Lat 18° 50N, 168°48’W.

The plan is to do a 3 body sight to establish a fix. Determined

Temperature 25C, Pressure 1015 mb, Index error +1.5 minutes. First sight is Vega with a run of 3 sights

UTC Date and Time: 2025-07-16 06:30:15. Avg Hs (altitude) 37° 50’. DR position @ 0630: 18°51.6N, 168°45.9W. It was plotted based on the Vessel’s log (distance) and Course.

Take a 2nd body “Menkat” about 5 1/2 minutes later (run of 3 sights) Then a 3rd body ”Dubhe” about 8 minutes later (run of 3 sights)
