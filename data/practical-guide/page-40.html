Enter the three measured angles

Lunar Distance Use Case 1 “Sun-Moon” Aug 18, 2025 11:58 6. Input Lunar Sights into Celestial Navigation Plugin

Input the Measurements For Lunars, there are 3 angle measurements to input. The angle between the Moon and the Celestial Body, the Altitude of the Moon (w/Limb) to the Horizon. The Altitude of the Body (only the Sun has a Limb) to the Horizon.

Type: select Lunar. Celestial Body: select Sun

  • Also available are Planets,
  • 57 Navigation Stars and Polaris. 9 Stars known as Lunar Stars (see page 14 for more about a good selection). Select Limb: select Near or Far
  • Refer to the next illustration for examples of Near or Far.
  • The body is Near when the closest edge of a celestial body (Sun, Star, Planet) is brought to the nearest visible edge of the moon.
  • The body is Far when bringing the nearest edge of the body across the moon’s face to the far side of the moon. Measurement. Input Lunar Distance Angle between the Moon and the Celestial Body.
