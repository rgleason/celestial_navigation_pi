How to Guide for Celestial Navigation Plugin.

Introduction

The plugin helps determine your position (Latitude, Longitude) through sextant "altitude" observations of the Sun, Moon, planets, or stars. The

plugin can work together with an OpenCPN DR Route (Track), GPS Track, or without a route. The DR track could have been generated by

Weather Routing. Celestial Navigation serves as a reliable independent backup when GPS is unavailable, spoofed, or fail during long ocean

voyages. It can also be used to supplement GPS as an independent source. It displays a Circle of Position (COP) for each sight. On a small

area scaled plot, COP appears as a Line of Position. It calculates Intercept and Azimuth, calculates and displays a FIX from multiple celestial

body sights. And will calculate a Running Fix by advancing Lines of Position based on Vessel distance traveled and course between sights.

Notably new to release 2.8.x are the following

UTC time capture. Freeze the UTC clock display at the Sight’s “Mark”. UTC date and time can be copied into a new or existing sight. •

Sun, Moon, Planet and Star Finder” which can also be used to identify celestial body location and potential 2 and 3 body sights for a fix •

Event Calendar (Sunrise, Sunset, Twilight, etc.) to help you plan for your next set of sights at a projected location/ETA. •

An abbreviated Nautical Almanac. •

A Sight evaluation tools useful to assess the quality of a Run of Sights, identify sight outliers, or used to assess your personal sight error •

Coastal Navigation using a Sextant. Vertical Angle of a known Landmark to develop Circle of Position, and Horizontal Angle between 3 •

landmarks to develop a fix based on angles between the 3 landmarks.

Circle of Position plotted based on a Bearing of Sunrise or Sunset. A low precision method to estimate position without Sextant. •

Lunar Distancing Lunars Planning tool “Star/planet/moon/sun Finder” geared towards selecting good pair of bodies for a Lunar Sight. •

Collimation. Measure the Sextant's Index arm’s accuracy by measuring the angle between 2 stars and comparing to their known angle. •
