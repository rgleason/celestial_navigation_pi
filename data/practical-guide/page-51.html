7. Sensitivity Analysis (for time)

The 59°18.8’ Lunar measurement wasn’t very accurate. To demonstrate through Sensitivity analysis, change the LD sight from

59°18.8’ to 59°18.0’. That’s almost an arc-min difference. Select the Lunar you just did, and use Duplicate. Then Edit the

duplicate Lunar. Change the LD Measurement to 59°18.0’. It is an almost perfect LD measurement. Longitude error will be .1 arc

min, despite the 2 body sight has insufficient spread for a fix.

Next change the LD Measurement to 59°18.2 (0.2’ error). A very good lunar sight, not perfect. Clock correction is -19s (the 2

estimates rounded). UTC Time is earlier by 19s. 19s is about 4.75 minutes of Longitude (15°/hr, .25’/sec) based on Sun time.
