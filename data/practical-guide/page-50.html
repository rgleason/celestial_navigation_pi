Longitude - Historically, Longitude was the objective (after TIME). In 2.8.x, Latitude and Longitude is derived using the Moon and the

Body. The 46° spread of the 2 body fix is shown in description. A spread between 60° to 120° is a valid Fix. The narrow spread might

still be in the ballpark for Longitude. The accuracy of Lunars with the best Sextant and perfect to near perfect measurement is roughly

10-20 seconds, maybe 3nm. Historically, Navigators used a meridian transit to determine an accurate Latitude but not Longitude.

Because the Sun’s “hang time” at noon, made Longitude not accurate. They trusted their DR skills between the Meridian Transit Sight

and the Lunar Sight. And if the Sun-Moon Lunar was a relatively short time from the Meridian Transit, They determined Longitude

based on an assumed Latitude which they maintained by DR. Today, when we do Lunars, Lunar experts stop at UTC time because we

have additional capabilities. But from the 18th to mid 19th century, Longitude was the reason for doing Lunars.
