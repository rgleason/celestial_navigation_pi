1. Create Sight: Sight Properties > Sight Tab Use Case 1 SW of Hawaii UTC 16 Jul 2025

Open illustration at full size

Press New Button

Sight Measurement (Hs) is an angular measurement of an astronomical body using a sextant. It can be one of these types:

  • Altitude: Angle of the body above the horizon (most common method)
  • Lunar: angle from the moon to another body for TIME and assessing Watch error (number of min-sec fast or slow).
  • Azimuth: (Future. Some framework is available) Celestial Body: select the Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, and 57 Navigation Stars plus Polaris that are visible in either the Northern or Southern Hemisphere. Limb: For altitude shots of the Sun and Moon, choose lower limb unless obscured; then choose upper limb. Sight Measurement (Hs): Enter the sextant reading. 3 angle formats are supported: Degrees minutes, Degrees Minutes Seconds, Decimal Degrees. After input, the angle redisplay is per Open CPN's display settings. Back-sights are indeed supported. Minutes of certainty determine the width of the plotted circle or line of position COP/LOP. If too small, it is hard to see. 0.1 min. is visible after zooming into a small area chart. 10+ min. visible after zooming out to a large area chart. Try the +/- on Chart Scale
