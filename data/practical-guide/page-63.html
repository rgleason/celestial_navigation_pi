How to pre-setup your sextant index to make it easier to do a Lunar Sight?

It is sometimes difficult to bring the Celestial Body across to the Moon. A simpler approach is to predict the LD angle•

for a given time and Lunar Pair, and set your sextant to that LD angle.

Then, sight the Moon. With a little rotation of your sextant, you’ll see the Celestial object near the moon.•
