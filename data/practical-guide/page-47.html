6. Input Lunar Sights into Celestial Navigation Plugin

Time (UTC)

Input your chronometer’s time in Coordinated UTC like normal Altitude Sights.

Certainty has always been part of the Cel Nav plugin, a very nice feature. For

altitude sights, the Circle of Position line width is the Measurement uncertainty. The

2 thin lines drawn down the middle of the COP plot denote Time Certainty. The

Lunar solution (v 2.4.66 and v 2.8x) does not produce a Circle of Position plot.

For lunar sights, this field sets the total interval searched for UTC. A new sight

defaults to 86,400 seconds, searching ±12 hours around the entered watch reading.

Normally leave this unchanged. Older saved sights may retain a smaller span. Unlike

the older interpolation calculation, narrowing this interval does not refine the

calculation: it excludes potential solutions outside the selected interval.

If the old Lunar sight doesn’t solve, expand the certainty to 86,400 seconds or more.
