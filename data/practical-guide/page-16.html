3. Fix or Running Fix (Traditional Cel Nav Method “Each sight’s DR Shift”)

View full-resolution illustration

COP/LOP display and the Big X while Fix form is open

The Fix form temporarily places a Big Red X at the Fix (Running Fix). When the Fix form is closed, the Big Red X disappears.

If maintaining a DR route, Drop a Mark (say a circle) at the Big X, so you have record of it on the chart. It is up to you if you want to restart the Route form the Fix. General guide line is that you don’t if the FIX is close enough to the DR Track / Route.
