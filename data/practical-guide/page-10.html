Set sight parameters

Use Case 1 SW of Hawaii UTC 16 Jul 2025

Open illustration at full size

1. Create Sight: Sight Properties > Parameters Tab

To use the same Parameters for future sights, input parameters and then press, “Set as Defaults” button.

For a natural horizon sight, HOE and Index Error is crucial. optionally Temperature, Pressure.

For Dip short sights, click on the Dip Short radial button, and input the distance in nautical miles from your DR to the shoreline where the celestial body will or was brought down to. Use OpenCPN chart to measure the distance and bearing. To remove Dip short, zero out the distance and unblock the radial button.

For Artificial Horizon sights, click on or off the radial button.

Remarks is new to v2.9.x. Input text remarks about the sight you are taking. Remarks display on the Sights form
