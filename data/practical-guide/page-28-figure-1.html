Use Case 2 Horizon Event…Sunrise

It is difficult for offshore cruiser to identify precise time and bearing of Sun UL/LL Sunrise or Sunset. On the other hand, professionals have better tools that give much better results.

The Sunrise observed at 16:51:00. Bearing 065° (True)

Note the uncertainties used in this example.

  • Time uncertainty : 10 seconds (better if using Gyro with Azimuth ring which has a prism)
  • Bearing uncertainty : 5° with a hockey puck compass (Gyro is better)
  • Refraction at horizon uncertainty : 0.3 arc minutes.
