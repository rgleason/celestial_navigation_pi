Use Case 1 SW of Hawaii UTC 16 Jul 2025 1. Create Sight: Sight Properties > Sight > Find

Position source drop down menu Default value is Boat Position.

Latitude and Longitude defaults to Boat’s position. If GPS is attached to OpenCPN, Lat/Lon will be the defaulted but the boat is moving. So, DR will constantly change. You’ll need to freeze it for the DR position of the Sight. Then you are done. But, if you don’t have GPS, drop a waypoint where the sights were taken. Then on the Find form, You can select that waypoint for the sight’s DR position.
