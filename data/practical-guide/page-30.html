Horizontal coastal sights

9. Position using Horizontal Sextant Sights of Landmarks or Waypoints

  • A useful capability for Offshore Navigation when approaching the Coast.
  • Before approach, while studying the Coastal Chart identify potential sights
  • Drop DR for the Landmarks (e.g. Lighthouses) in OpenCPN. Position, Light pattern, Light Range, Height (from Light to Sea)
  • Because ENC charts lack sufficient “landmark” detail in Maine.
  • Besides Sextant, Radar can also be used with this feature.
