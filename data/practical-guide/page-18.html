3. Fix or Running Fix (Traditional Cel Nav Method “Each sight’s DR Shift”)

COP/LOP display and the Big X while Fix form is open

Notice the impact of DR Shift

on the Fix (About 1.3 nm shift)

As the cursor hovers over a Circle of

Position, the Sight information is displayed.

C255 (Vega sight is green)

S 6.0

The Fix form temporarily places a Big Red X at the Fix (Running Fix). When the Fix form is closed, the Big Red X disappears.

If maintaining a DR route, Drop a Mark (say a circle) at the Big X, so you have record of it on the chart. It is up to you if you want to

restart the Route from the Fix. A few considerations in no particular priority. The FIX is close enough to your DR Position (Track /

Route), GPS, Fix confidence (Cel Nav Plug displays COP uncertainty), consistent with previous Sights/Fixes, wind/current change,

nearness to land or rendezvous, and more. What would you consider? What would be your decision?
