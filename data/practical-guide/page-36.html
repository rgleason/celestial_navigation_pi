5. What are the Process steps to do Lunars on board Ship? You should be doing 2 altitude sights of one body, 4 lunar sights, 2 altitude sights of the Moon. Sequence matters. 1. Measure One (1) Altitude Sight of the Moon.

2. Measure One (1) Altitude Sight of the Celestial Body.

3. Measure Four (4) Lunar Distance Angle of the Moon to your selected Celestial Body.

4. Hold the Sextant at an angle. The horizon mirror point to one object, and index mirror pointed to the other. Make sure the mirror that points to the sun has shades on to protect your eyes. Measure “edge” to “edge”.

5. Measure One (1) Altitude Sight of the Celestial Body. Note the reverse order!

6. Measure One (1) Altitude Sight of the Moon.

7. Then average the Lunar Distance Angle and UTC Time. Average the Moon Altitude Sight and UTC. Average the Celestial Body Altitude Sight and UTC. After averaging, the 3 sets of sights should have roughly the same UTC.

8. Input the average measurements into the Celestial Navigation Sights form. Verify Index Error, Height of Eye, Temp/ Barometer (minor impact) in the Parameters form.

9. Input your DR Position (Open CPN’s “Boat position” is the default, which should be changed to freeze it. This is found in the Find form.

10. Input the average Ship’s UTC in the Cel Nav Time (UTC) form.

11. Press the “TIME” Button in the Sights form to see the results.
