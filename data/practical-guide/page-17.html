4. Mark UTC Time of the sight (new for 2.8.8)

A new Cel Nav 2.8.5.9 supports the process where the observer operates the Sextant and calls out Mark. When calling out Mark, the recorder press the Mark button. UTC and Local PC Time clock display is frozen. Then press “Copy marked UTC”. Create a New or Edit and existing sight. Enter the Sight Data, move forward to the Time Tab and press the “Use marked UTC….” to over write the UTC Date and Time. You can still edit the UTC Date and Time
