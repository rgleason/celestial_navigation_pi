The Sun-Moon worked example

Lunar Distance Use Case 1 “Sun-Moon” Aug 18, 2025 11:58

The following is an example of a Sun-Moon Lunar Sight. Sun-Moon Lunars were known to be one of the best picks for UTC time and longitude. Historically, the Moon at 50% illumination was considered the best for various reasons. Through this example, we’ll demonstrate the Celestial Navigation Plugin features. And after that, we’ll show how to determine good Lunar Candidates.
