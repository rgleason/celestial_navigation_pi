8. Alternative your Lunar for position (OPTIONAL)

The Lunar Sight does not plot 2 circle of positions.

To wish to visualize the impact of the UTC change, copy the Lunar sight and turn them into 2 Altitude Sights. The 2 altitude sights will plot circle of positions.

Do this by the following. Select a Lunar sight (the Lunar with 18.2’ measurement), Click on the Eyes. Then press Duplicate 2 times to create 2 additional sights (see below). Select one of the duplicates, Press EDIT and then update Type to Altitude change Celestial Body, Limb, Measurement, and even Certainty corresponding to the Lunar’s “Body”. Change UTC time to 11:57:41 which is the Lunar corrected time. Then do the same for the other duplicate.
