5b. Process Steps to do Lunars on Land (continued)

One of the positives about learning Lunars is that you can do Lunar sights in your backyard without measuring an altitude sight.

  • Do it during the day with the Sun and Moon.
  • Do it in the middle of the night when you can’t see the visible horizon.
  • We’ll next describe how to do it.

5c. The Process Steps to do Lunars in your backyard

You can eliminate Steps 1, 2, 5, and 6. You need to do Step 7 and 8 for the 3+ sets of sights. When inputting Altitude Sights for the Moon and for the Celestial Body, use Celestial Nav plugin’s Estimated (Hs) Altitude and for UTC time, use the Lunar average UTC. But you still need to input your Latitude and Longitude so that the Altitude (Hs) of the Moon and Celestial Body will calculate. Lunar Distance calculation relies on the Parallax and Refraction which is derived from the 2 bodies. There is no DIP in Lunar Measurement. The Moon and it’s pair needs to be above the horizon for the computation.

In Use Case 1 we’ll demonstrate how to input Lunars into the Cel Nav Plugin, and we’ll indicate what to do for a “Backyard” Lunar.
