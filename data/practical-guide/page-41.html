5c. The Process Steps to do Lunars in your backyard

You can eliminate Steps 1, 2, 5, and 6. You need to do Step 7 and 8 for the 3+ sets of sights. When inputting Altitude

Sights for the Moon and for the Celestial Body, use Celestial Nav plugin’s Estimated (Hs) Altitude and for UTC time,

use the Lunar average UTC. But you still need to input your Latitude and Longitude so that the Altitude (Hs) of the

Moon and Celestial Body will calculate. Lunar Distance calculation relies on the Parallax and Refraction which is

derived from the 2 bodies. There is no DIP in Lunar Measurement. The Moon and it’s pair needs to be above the

horizon for the computation.

In Use Case 1 we’ll demonstrate how to input Lunars into the Cel Nav Plugin, and we’ll indicate what to do for a

“Backyard” Lunar.
