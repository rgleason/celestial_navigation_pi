Use Case 2 Horizon Event…Sunrise

It is difficult for average offshore cruiser to identify Time and bearing of Sun UL/LL Sunrise or Sunset. On the other hand, professionals have better tools that give much better results.

The Sunrise observed at 16:51:00. Bearing 65°

Note the uncertainties used in this example.

  • Time uncertainty : 10 seconds (skill of navigator. Much better if using Gyro with Azimuth ring which has a prism)
  • Bearing uncertainty : 5 degrees (hockey puck compass..Gyro is better)
  • Refraction Uncertainty : 0.3 arc minutes.
